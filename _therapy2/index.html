About the practice

How the work actually runs

Sessions run 50 minutes, usually weekly to begin with and moving to fortnightly as things settle. You set what gets discussed and when — nothing is pushed before you are ready for it.

What to expect early on

  • A free 15-minute call first, to check this is the right fit before anything is booked.
  • A first session spent mapping what you want to be different, not recounting history.
  • An agreed pace, reviewed openly as you go rather than assumed.
  • A referral onward if someone else is genuinely the better fit.

Training and approach

Licensed professional counselor with certification in EMDR and training in Internal Family Systems, alongside CBT, DBT and Gottman method for couples. Which of those gets used depends on what you bring, not on a fixed house style.
